# SnakeSmartHammer

The smart hammer on the regular vanilla hammer: grids, Hold, nudging and the **K** panel, plus
saved blueprints with pictures. Nothing changes until you use one of its keys. Client side only;
works with Valheim Plus's grid alignment on or off.

- Press **Y** with the hammer out for the **blueprint list** (with a small picture of each one):
  click a name or picture to build it, type to search, delete with a second click to confirm.
- To make a blueprint, aim at built pieces and press **F** (Shift+F: the whole group they were
  built with, Alt+F: everything connected) or use the **J** box, then press **Save selection** in
  the list (or Ctrl+S) and name it.
- Saved blueprints also get their own **Blueprints** tab in the build menu, after Favorites (Q/E
  or click), with their pictures as icons.
- A blueprint that needs more materials than the build info's slots lists all of them (have /
  need, red when short) in a panel beside or above it.
- **Dual wielding spears**: no grid, blueprint or nudge will put a spear in each of your hands.

## Installing

- Install for each player who wants it. It doesn't need to be on the server.
- **Remove SnakeHammer or SmartBuild**: while either is installed this mod stays off.
- Coming from SnakeSkills 1.28 or older (where the smart hammer was built in): keep SnakeSkills
  and add this mod. On its first start it copies your smart hammer settings from SnakeSkills'
  config. Blueprints stay in `BepInEx/config/SmartBuild/Blueprints`.
